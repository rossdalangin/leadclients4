# GrowthPress OS: The Comprehensive Operations Playbook v6.3 Definitive

This manual provides granular instructions on **how, when, and where** to manage every asset within the GrowthPress ecosystem. Treat this as your "Business OS Flight Manual."

---

## 🏎️ Core Lead & Sales Velocity

### 1. Leads (`gp_lead`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Leads
*   **When**: Automatically captured via `[gp_lead_form]` or `[gp_quiz_lead_form]`.
*   **How**: Monitor the **Kanban Board** on the Strategic Dashboard. Every new inquiry is triaged by AI, assigned a deal probability score, and tagged (e.g., "HOT").
*   **Management**: Open a lead to view the **AI Intelligence Brief**. Use the "Suggest Reply" feature to handle objections before you even pick up the phone.

### 2. CRM Kanban Sales Command
*   **Where**: GrowthPress Dashboard
*   **When**: Daily Sales Huddle / Action Planning.
*   **How**: Drag cards between columns (New -> Qualified -> Booked -> Closed).
*   **Management**: Clicking a card opens the "Neural Dossier." Moving a lead to "Booked" triggers the Appointment automation.

### 3. Tasks (`gp_task`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Tasks
*   **When**: Generated automatically by the **AI Lead Analysis** (e.g., "Follow up with John regarding ROI projections").
*   **How**: Review the Task Meta Box inside each Lead.
*   **Management**: Mark tasks as "Completed" to keep your Pipeline Velocity high.

### 4. Appointments (`gp_appointment`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Appointments / Dashboard Widget
*   **When**: Triggered via `[gp_booking_form]` or manual entry.
*   **How**: Integrated with Google/Outlook calendars.
*   **Management**: Manage staff availability and location-specific slots here. Automated SMS reminders are triggered from this module.

---

### 5. Funnels
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Funnels
*   **When**: When launching a new ad campaign or lead magnet.
*   **How**: Create a Funnel object, link it to a Landing Page, and set the "Conversion Goal" (e.g., Lead Submission).
*   **Management**: The dashboard will track the "Funnel Velocity" (days from entry to close) for each specific funnel.

---

## 🏛️ Inventory & Authority Assets

### 6. Portfolio / Inventory (`gp_project`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Projects
*   **When**: After every successful client transformation or new inventory acquisition.
*   **How**: Create a Project, add "Before/After" images, and select the **Niche Accent**.
*   **Management**: Use the `[gp_smile_gallery]` (Dental) or `[gp_project_tracker]` (Contractor) shortcodes to display these on the frontend.

### 7. Case Studies & Projects
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Case Studies
*   **When**: To build high-authority social proof.
*   **How**: Link a Project to a specific Client Profile.
*   **Management**: AI can summarize these into "Market Success Stories" for your Blog.

### 8. Service Lines (`gp_service`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Services
*   **When**: During initial setup or when expanding offerings.
*   **How**: Add service details, pricing tiers, and AI-optimized SEO descriptions.
*   **Management**: These populate the "Services" page generated during the Niche Wizard deployment.

### 9. Knowledge Base (`gp_kb`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Knowledge Base
*   **When**: To reduce support labor and train the AI.
*   **How**: Add FAQs, educational guides, and authority articles.
*   **Management**: The **AI Chat Assistant** scans this database to answer visitor questions in real-time.

---

## 💰 Transactions & Financials

### 10. Proposals (`gp_proposal`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Proposals
*   **When**: Immediately after a discovery call.
*   **How**: Use the "Generate AI Proposal" button within a Lead to instantiate a branded PDF/Digital agreement.
*   **Management**: Track "Sent", "Viewed", and "Accepted" statuses. Acceptance triggers the Transaction module.

### 11. Transactions
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Transactions / WooCommerce
*   **When**: Upon proposal acceptance or booking deposit.
*   **How**: Integrated with Stripe/PayPal.
*   **Management**: View "Revenue Forecasts" on the main dashboard based on these successful conversions.

---

## 📍 Scalability & Reputation

### 12. Locations (`gp_location`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Locations
*   **When**: For multi-office businesses or agencies.
*   **How**: Add ZIP/Postcode coverage areas and staff assignments.
*   **Management**: Lead routing will automatically assign inquiries to the nearest location based on the visitor's IP or input.

### 13. Reviews (`gp_review`)
*   **Where**: Admin Menu > Reputation
*   **When**: Post-transaction or project completion.
*   **How**: Automated SMS/Email requests sent via Twilio/Mailgun.
*   **Management**: Approve and display 5-star reviews using the "Social Proof" widget. AI scans for "Negative Sentiment" to alert management before a review goes public.

---

## 🧠 System Intelligence Management

### 14. AI Prompt Lab
*   **Where**: Settings > GrowthPress > AI Prompt Lab
*   **When**: To refine the "Tone of Authority" for your specific niche.
*   **How**: Enter custom instructions (e.g. "Speak like a high-end defense attorney").
*   **Management**: Test the output in the sandbox before pushing live to the Chat Assistant.
