# GrowthPress Page Wireframes & Structural Layouts

This document describes the high-converting structural layouts for the core pages in the GrowthPress OS.

## 1. Homepage (The Authority Engine)
- **Header**: Sticky navigation with glassmorphism; High-contrast "Book Now" CTA.
- **Hero Section**:
  - Left: H1 (Elite Value Prop), Subheadline, Trust Badges (Forbes, Clutch).
  - Right: `[gp_quiz_lead_form]` embedded in a semi-transparent glass card.
- **Value Pillars**: 3-column layout highlighting AI Automation, Instant Booking, and Elite Results.
- **Dynamic Content**: Niche-specific hook (e.g., Solar ROI Calculator or Symptom Checker).
- **Social Proof**: `[gp_review_feed]` slider showing 5-star verified results.
- **Lead Magnet**: "Download Your Growth Roadmap" footer section.

## 2. Services Overview (The Solution Map)
- **Intro**: H1 (Industry Category), brief authority-building paragraph.
- **Service Grid**: Alternating 2-column sections (Image/Text) for primary treatments or service lines.
- **Staff Highlight**: Showcase of senior professionals with "Book with [Name]" direct links.
- **Sticky Sidebar**: `[gp_urgency_banner]` + Mini lead form.

## 3. Qualification Quiz (The Triage Page)
- **Center Stage**: Minimalist layout with focus on the multi-step quiz.
- **Progress Bar**: Animated blue bar showing qualification depth.
- **Micro-Copy**: "95% of our clients save $[X] after this quiz."
- **Terminal State**: Lead capture form + Instant booking suggestion.

## 4. Secure Client Portal (The Operations Hub)
- **Sidebar**: Quick links to Appointments, Proposals, Document Hub, and Billing.
- **Dashboard Grid**:
  - Top: "Welcome Back, [Name]" + "Your Next Appointment is in 2 Days."
  - Middle Left: "Active Proposals" with 'Accept' buttons.
  - Middle Right: "Secure Documents" (Lab results, Contracts).
  - Bottom: "AI Strategy Updates" from the assigned professional.

## 5. High-Ticket Landing Page (Direct Response)
- **Header**: Logo only (no navigation to reduce exit points).
- **Hero**: Aggressive headline + 60-second video placeholder.
- **The Offer**: Bulleted list of benefits + Scarcity alert ("4 slots left").
- **Terminal Action**: Large, high-contrast button triggering the Quiz or Intake Form.
