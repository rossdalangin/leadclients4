# Behavioral Psychology Glossary: GrowthPress Triggers

The GrowthPress Business OS implements deep behavioral science to increase authority and lead conversion.

## 1. Micro-Commitments (The Quiz)
Instead of asking for a phone number immediately, we use a **Multi-step Quiz**. By getting the user to answer 2-3 low-friction questions first (e.g., "What is your energy bill?"), they are more likely to complete the lead form due to the "consistency principle."

## 2. Anchoring (The Solar/Tax Calculators)
Our calculators anchor the "price" of a service against the "cost of inaction." By showing a lead they are losing $300/mo to a utility company, the investment in a solar system feels like a logical saving rather than an expense.

## 3. Scarcity & Social Validation (Dynamic Banners)
The `[gp_urgency_banner]` uses real-time constraints ("3 slots remaining for October") to trigger FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out). This is balanced by the `[gp_review_feed]`, which provides "Social Proof" to lower the perceived risk of high-ticket transactions.

## 4. The Authority Heuristic (Glassmorphism UI)
The "Luxury" design system—featuring high-weight typography and deep translucency—signals high status and modern expertise. This heuristic causes prospects to trust the business more quickly, justifying premium price points.

## 5. Speed-to-Lead (AI Triage)
By responding with an AI-personalized brief in under 5 minutes, we capitalize on the user's current "Problem Focus." The longer a lead waits, the more their emotional urgency decays.
