# GrowthPress UX & Conversion Strategy

## Core Philosophy
The objective is to move users from "Visitor" to "Qualified Lead" to "Booked Appointment" with minimal friction.

### Conversion Funnels
1. **The Hook**: AI-powered lead magnets (e.g., "Get an AI ROI Estimate").
2. **The Filter**: Conversational AI forms that qualify leads based on intent and urgency.
3. **The Commitment**: One-click calendar booking immediately after form submission.
4. **The Loop**: Automated SMS/Email reminders to reduce no-shows.

### Psychology Triggers
- **Authority**: High-quality case studies and "Smile Galleries" (for Dental).
- **Social Proof**: Real-time review feeds integrated into the UI.
- **Urgency**: Dynamic banners for emergency services (Roofing/Dental).
- **Trust**: Professional, clean, and fast-loading interface.

### Mobile-First Approach
All booking and lead capture components are optimized for thumb-reach and one-handed operation.
