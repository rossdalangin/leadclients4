# GrowthPress Niche Success Blueprints

How to use the GrowthPress Business OS for specific industry growth.

## ⚖️ Law Firm: The 'Speed-to-Justice' Strategy
- **The Asset**: Use `[gp_legal_intake]` on all case-specific landing pages.
- **The Play**: Set 'Hot Lead' threshold to 90. When a lead mentions "court date" or "accident", the AI will trigger a high-urgency alert.
- **The Close**: Use the **Client Portal** to securely upload evidence immediately after the first call to build commitment.

## ☀️ Solar Company: The 'ROI Arbitrage' Strategy
- **The Asset**: Embed `[gp_solar_calculator]` above the fold.
- **The Play**: Capture utility bill data in the intake quiz. Use the **AI Strategic Brief** to mention specific 25-year savings on the discovery call.
- **The Close**: Present the **$0-Down Financing** options directly within the AI-generated proposal.

## 🦷 Dental Clinic: The 'Smile Authority' Strategy
- **The Asset**: Sync the **Smile Gallery (gp_smile_case)** with the Services page.
- **The Play**: Use the **AI FAQ Assistant** to answer Invisalign-specific questions 24/7.
- **The Close**: Enable the **Insurance Verifier** in the portal to remove financial friction before the first consultation.

## 🔨 Contractor: The 'Visual Trust' Strategy
- **The Asset**: Use `[gp_contractor_estimator]` for instant kitchen/bath quotes.
- **The Play**: Maintain a high-resolution **Portfolio (gp_project)**.
- **The Close**: Use the **Project Tracker** in the Client Portal to show current construction velocity, justifying premium labor rates.

## 📈 Coaches & Consultants: The 'Scalable Authority' Strategy
- **The Asset**: Deploy the `template-strategy.php` landing page.
- **The Play**: Generate weekly market updates in the **Knowledge Base (gp_kb)** using the Content Studio.
- **The Close**: Automate **Webinar Registration** and use AI to identify the top 5% of webinar attendees for direct discovery calls.
