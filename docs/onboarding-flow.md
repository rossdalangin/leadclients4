# GrowthPress Elite: The 14-Second Onboarding Sequence v6.3

Welcome to the world's most advanced AI-powered Business Operating System. This guide maps your journey from a standard WordPress install to total market dominance using the Elite v6.3 architecture.

## 🟢 PHASE 1: INITIALIZATION (Seconds 1-14)
The initialization sequence transforms your database into a high-performance 14-node conversion engine.

1.  **Activate Core Assets**: Install the `growthpress` theme and `growthpress-core` plugin.
2.  **Trigger Launch Wizard**: Navigate to **Strategic Command**. Select your niche (e.g., Solar, Law, Medical).
3.  **Deploy Engine**: Click **"Apply Updates & Sync All Pages"**.
    *   *Result*: 11 high-fidelity pages generated, 14 Custom Post Types registered, and AI triage logic calibrated for your specific industry.

## 🔵 PHASE 2: CALIBRATION (Minutes 1-15)
Connect the "Neural Brain" of your OS to external intelligence and financial nodes.

1.  **Omni-AI Intelligence**: Go to **Settings > AI Providers**. Connect OpenAI, Claude, or local Ollama instances for multi-node failover.
2.  **Financial Ledger**: Enter your **Stripe keys** to enable real-time ROI tracking in the Strategic Hub.
3.  **Visual Identity**: Use `Appearance > Customize` to set your **Elite Branding**. Your primary color and global corner geometry will instantly propagate across all 14-CPT frontend grids.

## 🟡 PHASE 3: DATA DENSITY (Day 1)
Seed your ecosystem to understand the full relational lifecycle.

1.  **System Tools**: Navigate to **Settings > System Tools**. Click **"Generate Sample Data"**.
    *   *Result*: Your Kanban board, ROI charts, and Ecosystem Radar are instantly populated with high-fidelity, historically back-dated records.
2.  **The Quiz Node**: Deploy the `[gp_quiz_lead_form]` on your homepage. This uses multi-step behavioral psychology to qualify prospects while you sleep.
3.  **Review the Hub**: As leads flow in, check the **Intelligence Performance Hub** to track conversion velocity and earnable equity.

## 🔴 PHASE 4: MARKET DOMINANCE (Ongoing)
Scale your operations with autonomous content and high-stakes triage.

1.  **AI Content Studio v1.8**: Weekly, use the Studio to generate SEO blogs or clinical protocols. Use the **"Sync to Node"** feature to instantly propagate intelligence across the KB and Services line.
2.  **Strategy Execution**: Clients accept proposals via the **Secure Portal**, which autonomously initializes Project nodes and generates kickoff tasks.
3.  **Authority Radar**: Use the `[gp_ecosystem_radar]` to visualize your market dominance on public authority pages.
