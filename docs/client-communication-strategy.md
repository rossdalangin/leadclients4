# Client Communication Strategy: The Omnichannel OS

In high-ticket services, communication is the primary driver of trust. GrowthPress OS uses a multi-channel approach to ensure prospects feel cared for at every stage.

## 1. Missed Call Re-engagement (SMS)
- **The Gap**: 60% of high-ticket leads call while your team is busy.
- **The Solution**: GrowthPress detects the missed call and sends an immediate AI-personalized SMS: *"Hi [Name], we missed your call but are ready to help. Most [Niche] questions can be answered here: [Link to AI FAQ] or you can book a slot here: [Link to Calendar]."*

## 2. Intent-Based WhatsApp Nurture
- **Residential Leads**: Focus on family safety, long-term savings, and ease of setup.
- **Commercial Leads**: Focus on ROI, tax incentives, and business continuity.
- **Enterprise Leads**: Focus on custom integration, security, and dedicated account management.

## 3. The 5-Day "Authority" Email Loop
- **Day 1**: Custom Strategy Roadmap.
- **Day 2**: Social Proof & Transformation Stories.
- **Day 3**: Addressing the "Hidden Costs" of inaction.
- **Day 4**: The ROI Calculator Breakdown.
- **Day 5**: Final Scarcity Invitation.

## 4. Secure Client Portal (The Hub)
- **Transparency**: Clients should be encouraged to login to track their "Legal Case" or "Construction Project" status.
- **Commitment**: The more a client uses the portal to upload documents, the less likely they are to churn.
