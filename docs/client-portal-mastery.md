# Client Command Hub: Mastery Guide (v6.3 Elite)

Welcome to your dedicated Strategic Command Hub. This portal is your primary interface for tracking project velocity, executing agreements, and managing your referral network.

---

## 🔐 Authentication & Security
Access to this hub is secured via AES-256-GCM encryption. If your organization uses SSO (Okta/Azure), use the "Enterprise SSO Uplink" on the login screen.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Operational Velocity
Monitor the real-time execution of your active business nodes.
-   **Execution Bar**: Shows the percentage of your current phase completion.
-   **Active Nodes**: Lists the specific services or systems currently being deployed.

### 2. Strategy Execution Node
Your personalized AI-generated roadmap is displayed here as a task list.
-   **Action**: Check off completed milestones to synchronize your progress with your assigned specialist.

### 3. Strategic Terminal (Agreements)
View and digitally execute proprietary proposals.
-   **Terminal**: click "OPEN TERMINAL" to read the full document.
-   **Execution**: click "EXECUTE & INITIATE KICKOFF" to digitally sign and begin the next phase of operations.

### 4. Interactive Financial Ledger
A real-time record of all settled and pending transactions.
-   **Tracking**: View investment amounts, status (Paid/Pending), and internal reference IDs.
-   **Statements**: Download a cinematic PDF statement for your corporate records.

---

## 🤝 Referral Hub (Monetization)
Propagate your success and earn rewards.
-   **Unique Link**: Share your link (`?ref=ID`) with your network. Anyone who inquiries through this link is automatically tagged as your referral.
-   **Manual Submission**: If you are making a warm introduction, input their name and email in the manual submission box.
-   **Referral Ledger**: Track the "Converted" status of your referrals and your "Paid" payout history.

---

## 📞 Strategic Uplink (Support)
Need direct assistance?
-   **Transmit**: Use the "Direct Strategic Uplink" form to send an encrypted message to your assigned specialists.
-   **History**: Previous meeting logs and Zoom recordings are stored in the "Intelligence Logs" section.
