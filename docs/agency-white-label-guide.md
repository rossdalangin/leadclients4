# GrowthPress Elite: Agency White-Label & Distribution Guide

GrowthPress is designed from the ground up to be white-labeled for agency distribution. Follow this guide to transform the system into your own proprietary Business Operating System.

## 🛠️ Step 1: Branding the Core Plugin
Transform the look and feel of the GrowthPress admin experience to match your agency's identity.

1.  **Brand Identity**: Navigate to **GrowthPress Settings > White-Label Settings**.
2.  **Brand Name**: Change "GrowthPress" to your agency's product name (e.g., "ApexOS"). This will update the Dashboard headings, AI Assistant name, and menu labels.
3.  **Dashboard Logo**: Provide a URL to your custom logo (recommended: 200x50px transparent PNG).
4.  **Primary Color**: Set your primary brand color. This propagates to the AI status icons, Kanban accents, and all frontend buttons.

## 🎨 Step 2: Customizing the Elite Experience
Use the built-in hooks and CSS injection points to provide a bespoke experience for your clients.

*   **CSS Injection**: You can inject custom CSS via the standard WordPress Customizer.
*   **Niche Calibration**: When onboarding a new client, use the **Launch Wizard** to generate pages tailored to their industry. You can then further refine the copy via the AI Content Studio.
*   **API Management**: Manage your clients' OpenAI and Twilio usage directly from the **Intelligence & Usage** section in Settings.

## 🔑 Step 3: License & Deployment
Manage your agency deployments with ease.

1.  **License Activation**: Enter your Agency License Key in the settings to unlock "Enterprise" mode for your clients.
2.  **Credit Management**: Track AI credit usage per install. You can set "Hot Lead" thresholds (e.g., 85) based on the client's service tier.
3.  **Security**: Use the **Bearer Auth Token** to connect your clients' sites to external automation tools like Zapier or Make.com while maintaining high-security standards.
