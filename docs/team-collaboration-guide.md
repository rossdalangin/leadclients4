# Team Collaboration & Internal Workflow Guide

GrowthPress OS is designed to function as a unified command center for your entire team. Follow these protocols to ensure maximum lead velocity and operational clarity.

## 1. CRM & Kanban Protocol
- **Stage Movement**: Leads must be dragged to the correct Kanban stage (Qualified, Booked, Closed) immediately upon action completion. This triggers niche-specific backend automations.
- **Urgency Flags**: Any lead with a red border (AI Score > 80) must be contacted within 5 minutes of receipt.
- **Assignment**: Always ensure the `_assigned_staff` meta is updated so the correct rep receives task notifications.

## 2. Strategic Briefs & Internal Notes
- **Pre-Call Review**: Before any discovery session, reps must read the **AI Strategic Brief**. Do not ask the prospect questions that they have already answered in the qualification quiz.
- **Internal Notes**: Every interaction (calls, texts, thoughts) must be logged in the **Internal Team Notes** box on the lead screen. This creates a permanent, searchable audit trail.

## 3. Autonomous Task Management
- **Next Steps**: Review the AI-generated "Action Plan" for every new lead. Mark these tasks as completed in the **My Active Tasks** dashboard widget once done.
- **Escalation**: If a lead is stuck in 'Qualified' for more than 48 hours, escalate to a manager by adding an internal note and tagging them.

## 4. Multi-Staff Booking
- **Calendar Sync**: All team members must keep their WordPress user profile availability updated. The booking engine only displays slots where staff members are free.
- **Rescheduling**: If a client requests a reschedule via the portal, the assigned rep will see a flag in their Activity Feed. Action this within 2 hours.
