# Objection Handling Guide

## 🛡️ Strategic Usage & Delivery
> **Persona:** Sales Development Rep (SDR) or Closer.
> **Environment:** Mid-Call Pivot or Email Rebuttal.
> **Objective:** Transition from "Cost" to "Equity Acquisition". Frame the OS as an asset, not an expense.

## "It's too expensive."
Response: "Compared to what? A standard site costs $X but earns $0 in automation. GrowthPress replaces $500/mo in SaaS tools (Calendly, CRM, AI Writers) and pays for itself with just one extra closed lead."

## "I don't understand AI."
Response: "You don't have to. The AI works in the background—scoring your leads and writing your follow-ups. It's like having a world-class assistant that never sleeps."
