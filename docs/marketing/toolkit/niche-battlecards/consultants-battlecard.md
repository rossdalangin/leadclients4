# Niche Battlecard: Management Consulting Dominance

## 🏛️ Strategic Positioning
**Target**: Managing Directors of Boutique Consulting Firms.
**Key Pain**: "Consulting Latency" – The gap between a high-stakes operational inquiry and a qualified strategic roadmap.

## 🥊 Competitor Weaknesses
1. **Generic "Service" Themes**:
   - **Weakness**: They describe *what* you do, not *how* you win. They have no relational architecture to link Case Merits to Proposals.
   - **GrowthPress Counter**: "GrowthPress v6.3 is a **Unified Strategic Command**. We link every 'Discovery Node' to a 'Project Velocity' tracker, providing clients with 100% transparency."
2. **Disconnected Project Tools (Asana, Monday.com)**:
   - **Weakness**: They require clients to login to *another* separate platform, creating "Information Silos."
   - **GrowthPress Counter**: "We provide an **Integrated Client Portal**. Your proposals, financial ledgers, and project milestones exist in one secure, white-labeled environment."

## 🚀 The "Unfair Advantage" Pitch
"Consulting is a high-stakes authority game. GrowthPress provides the **Technological Infrastructure** that makes your boutique firm look and function like McKinsey, at a fraction of the operational overhead."
