# High-Ticket Case Study Template

Use this structure to document client successes and build authority in your niche.

## 1. The Challenge (The Problem)
- What was the client's situation before GrowthPress?
- Example: "Dr. Miller was spending 10 hours a week on manual lead callbacks and losing 60% of prospects to faster competitors."

## 2. The Solution (The System)
- Which GrowthPress features were deployed?
- Example: "We installed the Dental OS with the AI Triage assistant and automated 5-day nurture sequence."

## 3. The Implementation (The Shift)
- How did the team's workflow change?
- Example: "The front desk now only calls leads with an AI Score > 80, prioritizing high-value Invisalign inquiries."

## 4. The Results (The ROI)
- Quantifiable metrics of success.
- Example: "In 90 days, bookings increased by 45%, and the 'Time to First Contact' dropped from 4 hours to 4 minutes."

## 5. The Conclusion (The Future)
- A quote from the client and the next phase of growth.
