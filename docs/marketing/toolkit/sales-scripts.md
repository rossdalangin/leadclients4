# High-Ticket Agency Sales Script: GrowthPress OS

## 1. The Hook (The "Systems" Problem)
"Most dental clinics (or law firms/contractors) have a website. But almost none have an **Operating System**. You're spending money on ads, but leads are falling through the cracks because your CRM, your website, and your booking system don't talk to each other."

## 2. The Solution (The Business OS)
"GrowthPress isn't just a theme. It's a fusion of Salesforce, Calendly, and OpenAI. It qualifies your leads using behavioral AI, routes them to your staff instantly, and handles the follow-up automatically. It’s like having a 24/7 sales manager who never sleeps."

## 3. The "AI Advantage"
"When a lead hits your site, our AI analyzes their sentiment. If they're urgent, it flags them. If they have questions about Invisalign or Solar ROI, it answers them instantly and pushes them toward a booking. You only talk to qualified, high-intent prospects."

## 4. Closing / Objection Handling
- **Objection:** "We already have a CRM."
- **Response:** "Is your CRM integrated with your website's behavioral tracking? Does it auto-generate proposals? GrowthPress replaces 5-6 different subscriptions and centralizes everything into WordPress."
