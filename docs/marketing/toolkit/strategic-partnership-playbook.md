# Strategic Partnership Playbook: Agency Growth via Associations

To scale past $1M ARR, agencies should stop selling 1-to-1 and start selling 1-to-Many via Strategic Partnerships.

## 1. Target Partners
- **Industry Associations**: American Dental Association (local chapters), National Association of Realtors, State Bar Associations.
- **Local Chambers of Commerce**: Positioning the GrowthPress OS as the "Digital Recovery Engine" for local business.
- **Supply Chain Partners**: Solar panel distributors or legal software providers who can "bundle" GrowthPress with their own services.

## 2. The Partner Pitch (The "Vetted" Narrative)
"We've built a specialized Business Operating System for [Niche] that replaces 5 fragmented tools and increases lead close rates by 40%. We're looking to provide a vetted deployment roadmap to your members as part of your technology benefit package."

## 3. The Joint Offer
- **Member-Only Discount**: 15% off the Setup Fee for association members.
- **The "Industry Benchmark" Report**: Provide a quarterly aggregate ROI report (anonymized) to the association to show the collective growth of members using the OS.

## 4. Co-Branded Marketing
- **Webinars**: "The Future of [Niche] in the Age of AI."
- **Knowledge Base Inclusion**: Partner allows you to contribute Authority Articles to their newsletter, linking back to the **[gp_quiz_lead_form]**.
