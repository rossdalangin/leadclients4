# Agency Pricing Guide: Bundling the GrowthPress OS

How to bundle and price the Business OS for different high-ticket tiers.

## Tier 1: The "Digital Foundation" (SMB)
- **Target**: Single-location Dental, Law, or Contractors.
- **Includes**: OS Launch, AI Triage, CRM Kanban, Basic Analytics.
- **Pricing**: $2,500 Setup + $499/mo Maintenance.

## Tier 2: The "Growth Accelerator" (Growth Stage)
- **Target**: Firms spending $2k+ on monthly ads.
- **Includes**: All Tier 1 + 5-Day Nurture, Client Portal, AI Content Studio (4 posts/mo), A/B Testing.
- **Pricing**: $5,000 Setup + $1,499/mo Management.

## Tier 3: The "Enterprise Dominance" (Authority Stage)
- **Target**: Multi-location groups or high-net-worth consultants.
- **Includes**: All Tier 2 + Multi-location routing, Custom AI Persona, 8 Authority Guides/mo, Quarter-over-quarter ROI Reporting.
- **Pricing**: $10,000+ Setup + $2,999+/mo Strategic Advisory.

## Upsell Opportunities
- **Cold Lead Reactivation**: One-time fee of $1,500 to run the Reactivation Scout on their old database.
- **Agency CRM Training**: Half-day workshop for $2,500.
- **Custom AI FAQ Training**: $1,000 to feed the AI specific industry whitepapers.
