# Referral Engine Playbook: Automating Social Growth

High-ticket service businesses thrive on referrals. GrowthPress OS includes an automated "Referral Loop" within the client portal to turn every successful project into 3 new leads.

## 1. The Post-Closing Trigger
Once a proposal is marked as 'Accepted' and the project hits 100% completion in the **Project Tracker**, the AI triggers a "Referral Reward" notification in the client portal.

## 2. The Incentive Structure
- **Dental**: "$100 account credit for you and a friend's first cleaning."
- **Solar**: "$500 referral bonus upon their system activation."
- **Law**: "Priority access and 10% discount on future retainer services."

## 3. Automated Referral Capture
In the portal's sidebar, clients have a **"Recommend a Peer"** button.
- Clicking this opens a mini `[gp_lead_form]` pre-tagged with the current client's ID.
- The new lead is automatically assigned a +10 "Trust Bonus" to their initial AI Lead Score.

## 4. The Nurture Sequence (Referral Track)
New leads coming via the referral engine get a specialized 3-day email sequence:
- **Day 1**: "Your friend [Client Name] thought we could help you with [Niche]..."
- **Day 2**: "Why [Client Name] chose the GrowthPress framework."
- **Day 3**: "A private discovery call invitation (Priority Slot)."
