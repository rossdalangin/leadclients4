# Niche-Specific High-Ticket Sales Scripts

Use these tailored talking points for each niche supported by the GrowthPress Business OS.

## ⚖️ Law Firms (Personal Injury)
"The AI triage has flagged your case as high-urgency because of the date you provided. Most people don't realize that every day they wait, evidence disappears. I've setup a secure document link in your portal—can you upload the accident report now so our attorney can review it before your call in 30 minutes?"

## ☀️ Solar Companies
"I saw on your ROI report that you're currently losing about $240 a month to the grid. Over 25 years, that's $72,000. Our system identified a 30% federal tax credit that brings your net investment down significantly. Shall we walk through the financing options the AI calculated for you?"

## 🦷 Dental Clinics (Cosmetic)
"We reviewed your AI Smile Design query. Most of our Invisalign patients tell us their only regret was waiting so long. Our insurance verifier just confirmed your provider covers $[X] of the treatment. We have one priority slot left for a consultation this Thursday."

## 🔨 Contractors (Kitchen/Bath)
"Based on the square footage you entered in our estimator, we can deliver a premium quartz finish while staying within your target range. Our project tracker will give you a daily live feed of our progress, so there are zero surprises. Does your current contractor offer that level of transparency?"

## 🏥 Medical Clinics
"Your AI symptom assessment suggests a consultation with our specialist is the best next step. Instead of sitting in a waiting room, I can send you a secure telemedicine link through your portal right now. Does 2 PM today work for you?"

## 🏠 Real Estate
"The AI Matchmaker found 3 properties that hit every lifestyle point you mentioned, including that dedicated home office space. One of them is an off-market listing. I've added them to your dashboard. Shall we book a private virtual tour?"

## 📈 Coaches & Consultants
"Your quiz results show you're stuck at the 'Manual Labor' stage of scaling. The AI roadmap we generated for you identifies 3 specific automation gaps. I’d like to walk you through the 'Phase 2: Scale' milestones we've already outlined for your business."

## 🧾 Accounting Firms
"The AI Tax Estimator found $12k in potential deductions that most small businesses in your revenue bracket miss. We've prepared a 12-month strategy session in your portal. Can we jump on a call to finalize the plan before the next quarterly filing?"
