# GrowthPress Agency Monetization Roadmap

As an agency deploying GrowthPress for clients, your revenue comes from both the "Setup" and the "Ongoing Intelligence" you provide.

## Tier 1: The "Digital OS" Setup (One-Time: $2,500 - $5,000)
- **Deployment**: One-click Niche Wizard setup.
- **Customization**: Logo, Primary Color, and Hero Headlines.
- **Integration**: OpenAI, Twilio, and Stripe connection.
- **Onboarding**: CRM training for the client's sales team.

## Tier 2: AI Engine Maintenance (Monthly: $500 - $1,500)
- **AI Content**: 4 Authority Articles per month generated in the Studio.
- **Funnel Optimization**: A/B testing variations of the Lead Quiz.
- **Report Analysis**: Monthly strategy session reviewing the Executive Dashboard.

## Tier 3: Growth-as-a-Service (Monthly: $2,500+ or % of Pipeline)
- **Active Sales Coaching**: Using AI Talk Tracks to train their reps.
- **Omnichannel Automation**: Managing WhatsApp and SMS nurture sequences.
- **Performance Guarantee**: Fee based on "Qualified Leads" (Score > 80) delivered.

## Scaling with White-Labeling
- Use the **Global Branding** settings to remove "GrowthPress" and insert your agency's name.
- Charge a premium for the "Agency Dashboard" access.
