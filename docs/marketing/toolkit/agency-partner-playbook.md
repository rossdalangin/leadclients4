# Agency Partner Playbook: Scaling to $1M+ ARR with GrowthPress

This playbook provides a roadmap for agencies to build a high-margin, scalable recurring revenue business using the GrowthPress ecosystem.

## 1. Productizing Your Offer
Don't sell "Marketing". Sell a "Business OS".
- **The Core Offer**: "The [Niche] Profit Engine".
- **What's Included**: Deployment of the GrowthPress OS, AI Triage calibration, and a 12-month Growth Roadmap.
- **Setup Fee**: $2,500 - $7,500.
- **Monthly Retainer**: $500 - $2,500.

## 2. The Setup Velocity (Profit Margin)
Standard agencies take 4-8 weeks to build a site. With the GrowthPress **Niche Launch Wizard**, you can deploy a 90%-complete system in 10 minutes.
- **Your Edge**: High efficiency means you can handle 10x more clients with the same staff.

## 3. High-Value Retainer Services
Keep clients paying by providing ongoing intelligence:
- **AI Content Management**: 4 SEO-optimized authority guides per month using the Studio.
- **Funnel Optimization**: Monthly A/B testing of the Lead Quiz.
- **Strategic Advisory**: Monthly review of the Executive Dashboard and ROI Reports.

## 4. Scaling with Multisite
Use the **WordPress Multisite** architecture (refer to `docs/system-scalability-multisite.md`) to manage hundreds of client instances from a single dashboard.

## 5. Sales Prospecting (The Audit)
- Offer a free "System Gap Audit".
- Show prospects how many leads they are losing due to slow response times.
- Contrast their static site with the **AI Strategic Brief** in GrowthPress.
