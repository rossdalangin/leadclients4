# High-Ticket Offer Architecture: Niche Specific

To maximize the GrowthPress Business OS, your core service offer must be structured for transformation, not labor.

## ⚖️ Legal: The "Priority Advocacy" Offer
- **The Core**: Aggressive representation for [Case Type].
- **The OS Multiplier**: Use the **Secure Case Tracking** in the portal to give clients 24/7 peace of mind.
- **Tactic**: Anchor the retainer against the potential settlement value identified in the **AI Lead Score**.

## ☀️ Solar: The "Energy Independence" Offer
- **The Core**: Full system design and 25-year installation.
- **The OS Multiplier**: Include the **AI ROI Projection** report as part of the initial proposal.
- **Tactic**: Position the $0-down financing as "Immediate Cash Flow Positive" using the **Solar Calculator**.

## 🦷 Dental: The "Confidence Reset" Offer
- **The Core**: Complete smile makeover (Veneers/Invisalign).
- **The OS Multiplier**: Show the **Smile Gallery** results via the automated nurture sequence.
- **Tactic**: Use the **Insurance Verifier** in the portal to show the client their out-of-pocket costs are lower than they think.

## 🔨 Contractor: The "Zero-Surprise" Renovation
- **The Core**: Kitchen or Whole-Home transformation.
- **The OS Multiplier**: Provide a **Real-Time Project Tracker** in the client portal.
- **Tactic**: Use the **Precision Estimator** to justify a higher-than-average labor rate through "Transparency and Accuracy."
