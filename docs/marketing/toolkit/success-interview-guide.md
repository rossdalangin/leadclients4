# Success Interview & Testimonial Guide

Building high-ticket authority requires real proof. Use these 5 questions to capture high-converting testimonials from your GrowthPress clients.

## 1. The "Response Gap" Question
*"Before we installed GrowthPress, how long did it usually take for you to respond to a new inquiry, and how many leads do you think you were losing?"*
- **Goal**: Highlight the initial pain point of the "Response Gap."

## 2. The "AI Triage" Question
*"What was your initial reaction to the AI deal probability and intent scoring, and how has it changed your sales team's daily workflow?"*
- **Goal**: Highlight the OS's intelligent operational engine.

## 3. The "Portal Transparency" Question
*"How have your clients reacted to having their own Secure Portal for case tracking and document management?"*
- **Goal**: Showcase the improved client experience and authority.

## 4. The "Quantifiable ROI" Question
*"In the first 90 days, what specific change have you seen in your booking rate or overall revenue?"*
- **Goal**: Get the "Hard Data" for your next Pitch Deck.

## 5. The "Recommendation" Question
*"If a peer in your industry was on the fence about moving from a traditional website to this AI Operating System, what would you tell them?"*
- **Goal**: The "Closer" for your marketing material.
