# Lead Nurture Playbook: High-Ticket Psychology

## 🧠 Strategic Usage & Delivery
> **Persona:** Marketing Automation Manager or Founder.
> **Environment:** CRM Workflow / Email Service Provider (ESP).
> **Objective:** Bridge the "Confidence Gap" between lead capture and discovery call. Use the AI Content Studio to generate the specific email copy based on these psychological pillars.

The goal of high-ticket nurture is to move the prospect from "Problem Awareness" to "Trust in the Solution" using AI-driven touchpoints.

## 1. The Reciprocity Trigger (Day 1)
Deliver the custom Roadmap immediately.
- **Why**: Cognitive science shows that when we receive value first, we feel a social obligation to return the favor (by booking a call).

## 2. The Logic of Proof (Day 2-3)
Use the **Smile Gallery** or **Case Studies**.
- **Why**: High-ticket buyers are risk-averse. They need to see that you have solved their exact problem for someone else.

## 3. The Authority Positioning (Day 4)
Refer to a **Knowledge Base** article.
- **Why**: Establish that you are an expert, not just a service provider. "We literally wrote the book on [Topic]."

## 4. The Loss Aversion Pivot (Day 5)
Anchor the price against the "Cost of Inaction" from the ROI Calculator.
- **Why**: Humans are more motivated by avoiding a loss than achieving a gain. "Every month you wait is $[X] in lost savings."

## 5. The Scarcity Bridge
Use the **Urgency Banner** status.
- **Why**: If you are available forever, you have no value. Limit slots to create a "Decision Deadline."
