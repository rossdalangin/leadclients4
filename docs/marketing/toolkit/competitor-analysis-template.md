## The "Efficiency Arbitrage"
A traditional site requires a human to spend 4-10 hours per week on follow-up and data entry. At $50/hr, that's $800 - $2,000 in monthly overhead. GrowthPress automates 80% of these tasks, paying for itself in the first 30 days.
