# GrowthPress High-Ticket Sales Playbook

## Speed to Lead: The Golden Rule
For high-ticket service niches (Law, Dental, Solar), the likelihood of closing drops by 80% after the first 5 minutes. GrowthPress AI triage is designed to help you identify "Hot" leads (Score 80+) instantly so you can react.

### The 5-Step Closing Framework

#### 1. The Immediate Acknowledgment (Automated)
GrowthPress sends an immediate AI-personalized SMS/Email acknowledgment. This "claims" the prospect and prevents them from browsing competitors.

#### 2. The Triage Review
Before calling, check the **AI Sales Insights** meta box on the lead screen.
- Review the **Deal Probability**.
- Use the **AI Closing Tactics** generated specifically for that inquiry.
- Memorize the **4 Discovery Questions**.

#### 3. The Discovery Call
Use the "Talk Tracks" from the AI Content Studio.
- **Goal**: Confirm the pain point identified by the AI.
- **Tactic**: "I saw you mentioned [Pain Point] in your inquiry. Most of our successful clients had that exact issue before we implemented our [Niche] framework."

#### 4. The Value Anchor
Instead of talking about "price", talk about "transformation".
- **Accounting**: "We aren't just doing your taxes; we are recovering $40k in lost capital."
- **Solar**: "You aren't buying panels; you are locking in a fixed energy cost for the next 25 years."

#### 5. One-Click Proposal
While on the phone, generate the proposal in the **Client Portal**. Ask the prospect to refresh their dashboard. Having them accept the proposal while you are on the line increases close rates by 40%.

## Objection Handling: AI Powered

### "It's too expensive."
**Response**: "I understand. Many of our clients felt that way until they saw the ROI Projection the AI generated for them. Based on your data, the cost of *not* acting is actually $[Estimated Loss] over the next 12 months."

### "I need to think about it."
**Response**: "Completely understand. High-ticket decisions require thought. However, our [Niche] schedule for this month is already 85% full (per our Urgency Banner). If we don't lock in your slot now, the next available kickoff is in 6 weeks."

## Daily Workflow for Sales Reps
1. **Login to Dashboard**: Check the Kanban 'New' column.
2. **Review Hot Leads**: Prioritize leads with Score > 80.
3. **Task Completion**: Complete the 3 "Next Steps" generated by the AI for every new lead.
4. **Move Stages**: Drag leads to 'Qualified' or 'Booked' immediately to trigger nurture automations.
