# Competitor Intelligence Brief: v6.3 Tactical Displacement

## Executive Summary
This brief identifies the "Fragility Nodes" of current market competitors. Use this intelligence to displace incumbent systems by highlighting GrowthPress's "Relational Integrity."

## Node 1: The "Manual Handoff" Fragility
- **Incumbents**: Calendly + Pipedrive + Mailchimp.
- **The Gap**: Data is lost or delayed when moving between these nodes. There is no unified "Brain."
- **The Displacement**: "Your systems aren't talking to each other. Every handoff creates latency. GrowthPress is a unified ecosystem where the booking engine *knows* the lead score, and the proposal engine *knows* the budget."

## Node 2: The "Passive Intake" Fragility
- **Incumbents**: WPForms, Gravity Forms, Typeform.
- **The Gap**: These tools just collect text. They don't *think*.
- **The Displacement**: "A form is a data dump. Our Neural Quiz is a triage node. It calculates intent and urgency in real-time, allowing you to prioritize $25k retainers over $50 inquiries automatically."

## Node 3: The "Reporting Delta" Fragility
- **Incumbents**: Manual spreadsheets or generic Google Analytics.
- **The Gap**: Most firms can't see their "Pipeline vs. Realized" equity without hours of manual calculation.
- **The Displacement**: "If you can't see your realized equity trajectory on a single dashboard, you're driving blind. We provide a real-time ROI Hub that tracks every dollar from inquiry to kickoff."
