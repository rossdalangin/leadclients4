# High-Ticket AI Nurture Sequence (5-Day Email)

This sequence is designed to be triggered automatically when a lead is captured but hasn't yet booked a discovery call.

## Day 1: The "Immediate Value" Delivery
**Subject:** [First Name], here is your custom [Niche] strategy...
**Body:** Thanks for reaching out. Our AI is already analyzing your inquiry. In the meantime, here is a guide we wrote on the top 3 mistakes [Niche] businesses make when scaling.
**Goal:** Build immediate authority and reciprocity.

## Day 2: The "Case Study" Proof
**Subject:** How [Client Name] achieved [Specific Result]
**Body:** I wanted to share a quick story about a client in a similar situation to yours. By implementing the GrowthPress roadmap, they were able to [Result] in 90 days.
**Goal:** Social proof and logical justification.

## Day 3: The "Pain Point" Deep Dive
**Subject:** Are you tired of [Common Niche Pain Point]?
**Body:** Most of our clients come to us because they are frustrated with [Pain Point]. Our AI-powered triage identifies these bottlenecks instantly. We can do the same for you.
**Goal:** Empathy and problem-focus.

## Day 4: The "ROI / Savings" Logic
**Subject:** The cost of waiting (Calculated)
**Body:** Based on industry data for [Niche], every month you wait to automate your [Process] is costing you roughly $[X]. Here is the breakdown of why our OS pays for itself.
**Goal:** Anchoring and loss aversion.

## Day 5: The "Hard CTA" / Scarcity
**Subject:** One last thing before our schedule fills up...
**Body:** We only take on 2 new high-ticket clients per week to ensure maximum attention. We have one slot left for this month. Book your discovery call now before it's gone.
**Goal:** Scarcity and final commitment.
