# Dental Clinic Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Market Positioning
Position the Dental OS as the "High-Ticket Smile Transformation Engine." The goal is to move the clinic from routine cleanings to high-value cosmetic cases (Invisalign, Veneers, Implants).

## 2. Target Channels
- **Instagram/Facebook Visual Ads**: High-resolution "Before/After" carousel ads linking to the **Smile Gallery**.
- **Local SEO**: Authority guides on "The ROI of a New Smile" and "Insurance for Invisalign in [City]."
- **In-Office QR Codes**: Prospects scan a code in the waiting room to start the **AI FAQ Assistant** or **Insurance Verifier**.

## 3. Creative Angles
- **The "Confidence Catalyst"**: "Your smile is your first impression. Use our AI Smile Design tool to see your transformation today."
- **The "Financial Friction-Free"**: "Most people wait because of cost. Our AI Insurance Verifier tells you your out-of-pocket in 60 seconds."

## 4. Offer Structure
- **The "Elite Smile" Launch**: $4,500 Setup + $1,200/mo management.
- **Includes**: AI Triage for cosmetic leads, Smile Gallery sync, and Automated Review generation.
