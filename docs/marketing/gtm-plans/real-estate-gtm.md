# GTM Plan: Real Estate Power Teams

## 🎯 Target Audience
Luxury home buyers/sellers and active investors looking for "off-market" inventory and data-driven guidance.

## 🟢 Phase 1: High-Intent Capture (Months 1-3)
- **Asset**: The `[gp_property_matcher]` (AI Lifestyle Matcher) to capture lifestyle intent before budget.
- **CPT Strategy**: Build a list of **Properties (gp_property)** with lifestyle-specific tags (Urban, Suburban, Coastal).
- **VSL**: "The 2024 Market Gap: How we find inventory Zillow doesn't have."

## 🔵 Phase 2: Nurture & Triage (Months 4-6)
- **AI Triage**: Inquiries analyzed for "Urgency" (e.g., must sell this month) and routed to lead agents.
- **Portal**: Secure client access to property dossiers, market reports, and strategy session notes.
- **Automation**: SMS "Property Alert" for matched intent tags in the CRM.

## 🔴 Phase 3: Regional Dominance (Months 7-10)
- **Content**: AI Content Studio used to generate weekly market reports for 5 target neighborhoods.
- **Reporting**: Track conversion velocity from 'Lifestyle Quiz' to 'Signed Representation'.
