# Law Firm Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: The Authority OS

## 1. Market Positioning
Position the Law Firm OS as "Secure, Aggressive, and Immediate." The value prop is reducing the time between a legal crisis and a professional consultation.

## 2. Target Channels
- **Local SEO**: Generate 10 guides in the **Knowledge Base** on "Personal Injury Statute of Limitations in [City]."
- **LinkedIn Targeted Ads**: Target Corporate Counsel and Business Owners with "The Secure Case Lifecycle" narrative.
- **Urgency Paid Ads**: Google Ads targeting high-urgency keywords (e.g. "car accident lawyer now").

## 3. Creative Angles
- **The "Evidence Gap"**: "Evidence disappears every hour you wait. Our AI Triage identifies high-urgency cases in under 60 seconds."
- **The "Client Peace of Mind"**: "Give your clients 24/7 access to their case status with our Secure Portal. No more late-night phone calls."

## 4. Offer Structure
- **The "Rapid Justice" Setup**: $5,000 Setup + $1,500/mo management.
- **Includes**: Secure Intake, Case Lifecycle Portal, and AI-Driven Follow-up.
