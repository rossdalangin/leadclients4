# GTM Plan: Management & Strategic Consulting

## 🎯 Target Audience
SaaS founders, mid-market CEOs, and operationally-strained professional firms ($1M - $20M annual revenue).

## 🟢 Phase 1: Intellectual Authority (Months 1-3)
- **Asset**: The `[gp_consulting_audit]` tool to capture high-intent operational data.
- **Library**: Populate the **Knowledge Base (gp_kb)** with 10+ AI-generated "Angle of Attack" competitive briefs.
- **Conversion**: Deploy the **Elite VSL** template for your core transformation offer.

## 🔵 Phase 2: Operations Scale (Months 4-6)
- **Portal**: High-stakes clients managing their 12-month roadmaps via the secure Client Portal.
- **CRM**: Use the **Strategic Brief** to identify cross-sell opportunities for leads based on sentiment analysis.
- **Nurture**: 5-day automated "Efficiency Blitz" email sequence.

## 🔴 Phase 3: Global/Regional Dominance (Months 7-10)
- **Whitelabel**: Use the Agency Mode to showcase your OS as a proprietary "Value-Add" to consulting packages.
- **Reporting**: Weekly Executive Summaries sent to clients showing project velocity and ROI gains.
