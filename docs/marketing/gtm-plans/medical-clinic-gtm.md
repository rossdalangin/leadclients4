# GTM Plan: Medical & Specialty Practice

## 🎯 Target Audience
Patients seeking specialty care (Orthopedics, Aesthetics, Wellness) who value clinical efficiency and digital-first communication.

## 🟢 Phase 1: Digital Triage (Months 1-3)
- **Asset**: The `[gp_symptom_checker]` (AI Health Intelligence) to reduce administrative intake burden.
- **Conversion**: Embed the **Staff-Aware Booking** engine on all treatment-specific pages.
- **Content**: "What to Expect: Your Specialized Care Journey" authority video in the VSL template.

## 🔵 Phase 2: Patient Lifecycle (Months 4-6)
- **Portal**: Secure patient access to results, educational material, and telemedicine links.
- **Automation**: SMS reminders with pre-visit intake forms to reduce 'no-show' rates by 30%.
- **CRM**: Track patient intent scores; high-intent aesthetic leads flagged for direct clinical outreach.

## 🔴 Phase 3: Authority Dominance (Months 7-10)
- **Reputation**: Automated review requests sent 24 hours after a 'Completed' appointment status.
- **SEO**: Dominate "Specialist [Treatment] Near Me" using AI-optimized treatment pages.
