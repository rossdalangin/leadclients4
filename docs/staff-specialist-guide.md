# Staff Specialist Guide: Operational Mastery v6.3

As a GrowthPress Specialist, you are responsible for the clinical triage and execution of strategic lead nodes. This guide outlines your primary workflows.

---

## 🚀 The Specialist Dashboard
Your dashboard is optimized for **Operational Focus**.
-   **Staff Performance Mode**: Tracks your active tasks and average response delta.
-   **Quick Actions**: Use the shortcuts to quickly create leads, tasks, or respond to chats.

---

## 📥 Lead Triage & Intelligence
When a lead enters the "Intake" node, follow this protocol:

1.  **Analyze Dossier**: Open the lead and click **"INTEL BRIEF"**.
    -   **Action**: Review the Deal Probability and Psychological Archetype.
    -   **Strategic Note**: Use the AI-generated "Suggested Reply" to maintain high-authority engagement.
2.  **Capture Sentiment**: Monitor the "Neural Intensity Heatmap." If engagement is high (>75%), initiate immediate outreach.
3.  **Sync Insights**: Once you've refined the strategy, click **"Sync to CRM"** to anchor the AI insights to the lead node.

---

## 💬 Chat Command (Real-time Engagement)
-   **Active Sessions**: Monitor the chat list for incoming inquiries.
-   **Keyword Templates**: Use pre-defined templates for Pricing or HIPAA queries to ensure 100% accuracy.
-   **Manual Uplink**: If the AI assistant cannot resolve a query, step into the session to provide manual specialist intervention.

## ✅ Global Task Center
All strategic actions are routed to the **Global Tasks** menu.
-   **Priority**: Focus on "High" priority tasks (marked in Red) first.
-   **ASAP Nodes**: Some tasks are generated by the AI upon intake—these are critical for the "5-Minute Response Rule."
