# GrowthPress OS: Master List of Processes

The GrowthPress Business Operating System automates the entire lead-to-cash lifecycle. Below is a comprehensive list of the core processes integrated into the ecosystem.

## 1. Lead Generation & Qualification Processes
- **Multi-Step Quiz Triage**: Behavioral-based qualification using the `[gp_quiz_lead_form]`.
- **AI Sentiment Analysis**: Immediate scanning of lead inquiries to detect urgency and high-ticket intent.
- **Dynamic Lead Scoring**: Automatic assignment of a 0-100 quality score to every new lead.
- **Priority Lead Routing**: Instant routing of leads scoring >80 to senior staff or administrators.
- **Lead Segmentation**: Auto-tagging prospects as 'Residential', 'Commercial', or 'Enterprise' based on their inquiry content.
- **Autonomous Action Planning**: System-generated 3-step action plans for every new lead, created as linked tasks in the CRM.

## 2. CRM & Sales Pipeline Processes
- **Kanban State Management**: Visual drag-and-drop movement of leads through New, Qualified, Booked, and Closed stages.
- **AI-Driven Next Steps**: Context-aware suggestions on each Kanban card (e.g., "Suggest Discovery Call", "Send Case Study").
- **Behavioral Journey Logging**: Tracking and displaying the specific pages a lead visited before and after opting in.
- **Task & Note Synchronization**: Centralized internal communication linked to specific lead records.
- **Proposal Generation**: One-click AI-powered high-ticket proposal drafting for leads in the sales pipeline.

## 3. Operations & Booking Processes
- **Staff-Aware Scheduling**: Intelligent booking engine that checks availability across multiple team members.
- **Telemedicine / Meeting Integration**: Automated generation of secure meeting links (Zoom/GrowthPress Health) upon appointment creation.
- **Multi-Branch Routing**: Automatically assigning leads to physical locations based on the prospect's ZIP code metadata.
- **Abandoned Inquiry Follow-up**: Hourly cron-based re-engagement for leads stuck in the 'New' stage for over 24 hours.
- **Appointment Reminders**: Automated SMS and Email notifications to reduce "no-show" rates.

## 4. Client Experience & Retention Processes
- **Secure Client Portal**: Gated access for clients to manage appointments, track project status, and upload documents.
- **Document Management**: Drag-and-drop secure upload and storage of sensitive files (Financials, Legal Intake).
- **Proposal Acceptance Workflow**: Digital signing and confirmation of service agreements within the client portal.
- **Automated Review Requests**: System-triggered requests for client feedback after successful appointment completion.
- **AI FAQ Assistance**: 24/7 conversational support with intent detection to upsell discovery calls.

## 5. Marketing & Content Processes
- **AI Blog Generation**: Production of niche-specific, SEO-optimized authority content via the Content Studio.
- **Omnichannel Sequence Drafting**: AI generation of 5-day Email and SMS nurture campaigns tailored to lead intent.
- **Market Angle Analysis**: Strategic competitor gap analysis to identify the best "Angle of Attack" for the niche.
- **Exit-Intent Capture**: Intelligent popups that trigger lead magnets based on user mouse behavior.
- **Dynamic Urgency Alerting**: Industry-specific banners (e.g., "3 Emergency Slots Left") to drive immediate action.

## 6. Executive Reporting & Management Processes
- **Growth Trend Analytics**: Real-time visualization of lead and booking volume over 30-day periods.
- **Team Performance Monitoring**: Tracking closed deals and productivity metrics for specific staff members.
- **ROI Estimation**: Automated calculation of project and lead value based on historical niche data.
- **System Activity Logging**: Auditable trail of every automated action, lead move, and AI generation.
- **Global White-Label Branding**: One-click rebranding of the entire admin and client experience.
- **Appointment Waiting List**: Automated priority queuing for prospects when calendar slots are at capacity.
- **AI Reactivation Scouting**: Periodic identification of dormant leads with context-aware re-engagement prompts.
- **Pipeline Value Calculation**: Automated summing of active proposal values for real-time revenue forecasting.
- **Behavioral Nudge Engine**: Delivery of psychologically-optimized sales tactics to reps during lead interaction.
- **AI Psychological Profiling**: Identification of prospect archetypes (e.g., Challenger, Implementer) to customize sales communication.
- **Market Dominance Benchmarking**: Real-time comparison of ecosystem saturation against sector-standard performance delta.
- **Strategic Recommendation Engine**: Autonomous generation of daily operational actions based on live system metrics.
- **Multi-Asset Campaign Orchestration**: Simultaneous generation of SEO, Email, and Ad copy assets in the Content Studio.
- **Niche-Aware Qualification**: Dynamic question adjustment in the intake quiz based on active industry selection.
